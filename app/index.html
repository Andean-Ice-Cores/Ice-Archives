Does removing tropical-mean warming change the ENSO signal in Andean ice cores?

δ18O, accumulation, dust and major ions from NOAA NCEI Andean ice cores compared with the Oceanic Niño Index (ONI) and NOAA’s Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI). All statistics are recomputed live in your browser.

ONI = 3-month running mean of Niño-3.4 (5°N–5°S, 170°–120°W) SST anomaly; ERSST.v6, centred 30-yr base periods updated every 5 yr.
RONI = 3-month running mean of [Niño-3.4 anomaly − 20°S–20°N mean SST anomaly], rescaled to the variance of Niño-3.4 (CPC, adopted Feb 2026).
Hypothesis — Andean δ18O partly records tropical-mean tropospheric temperature and moisture transport; if so, coupling should weaken under RONI.
